QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How do I install a theme?

Select Add theme on any theme to open it in NetNewsWire. You can also download the theme's zip from the latest GitHub release, unzip it and open the .nnwtheme file.

Are the themes free?

Yes. All ten themes are free and open source under the MIT License.

Do the themes work in dark mode?

Claudio, Franco, Micha, Maika, Sunjiyo and thechelsuk adapt to your device's light or dark setting. Fresh and Guro are light themes, and Magda and Retro are dark.

Can I preview a theme before installing it?

Yes. Select a theme's screenshot to open a full live preview of an article, including YouTube embeds and reference links.

Do YouTube videos play inside articles?

Yes. The themes embed YouTube videos through embed.thechels.uk, which supplies the referrer YouTube requires, so videos play inside NetNewsWire.

How do I change themes later?

You can switch theme at any time in NetNewsWire's settings.
