The joy of reading on your own terms

There is something quietly radical about choosing what you read. No algorithm deciding what matters, no feed trying to keep you scrolling — just the sites you picked, arriving in their own time.

RSS makes the web feel personal again. A handful of thoughtful writers, a few unexpected discoveries, and enough room to follow a thought all the way through.

Make room for the good stuff

A good reading app should get out of the way. NetNewsWire keeps your reading private, your subscriptions in your hands, and the articles right where you need them.

Read what you chose. In the order it came. At your own pace.

That is the whole idea — and a little care in the details makes it even better. More from the open web.

  • Follow curiosity, not a timeline
